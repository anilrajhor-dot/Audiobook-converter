How to use Lectern

1. Open a document

Drop a file onto the panel, or tap "Choose a file". Lectern reads PDF, EPUB, Word (.docx), plain text, and photos or scans (JPG/PNG etc.) — scanned pages are recognised automatically with on-device OCR, which can take a little longer. If a scanned page or photo is in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali or another Indian language, pick it from the "Scanned-page language" dropdown first — OCR needs to know the script to read it correctly. PDFs, EPUBs, Word files and plain text with a proper text layer work in any language automatically, no picker needed.

2. Listen

Once a book opens, the player bar appears at the bottom. ▶ plays from where the highlighted sentence is; ⏸ pauses and keeps your place; ⏹ stops and jumps back to the start of the current chapter. The ◀ ▶ arrows move a sentence at a time, and ⏮ ⏭ jump a whole chapter. Tap anywhere on the thin progress bar to skip to that point in the book.

3. Choose a voice

"Voice type" narrows the list to female, male, or child voices where your device offers them, then pick the exact voice from "Voice". Speed and pitch sliders adjust the reading as it plays. Whether Hindi, Tamil or other Indian-language voices appear here depends entirely on your device and browser — some have them built in, others need a language pack added in the phone's own settings. If none is available, the book's text is still there to read, just without a matching voice to speak it.

4. Your shelf

Every book you open is saved here in full, so you can come back and read it in parts over time — no re-uploading. Tap a title on the shelf to reopen it where you left off. The ✕ on a row removes just that book; "Clear finished" removes every completed book at once, and "Clear all" empties the whole shelf. All of this lives only in this browser on this device.

5. Home button

While reading, tap "🏠 Home" in the player bar to stop the audio and return to your shelf.

6. Studio narration (optional, paid)

If you'd rather have a real downloadable MP3 than the browser's voice, open "Studio narration", paste an OpenAI or ElevenLabs API key, and generate a chapter or the whole book. This only works once Lectern is hosted on a website — the in-chat preview blocks calls to outside services.

Good to know

  • Works fully offline once the page is loaded, except for Studio narration, which needs the internet.
  • Multi-column magazine layouts can read out of natural order.
  • On iOS Safari, switching apps mid-sentence can restart that sentence.
